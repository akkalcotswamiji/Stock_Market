FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS

Understanding a Business

Fundamental analysis studies a company's financial health and business performance.

  • P/E Ratio: Price compared with earnings per share.
  • EPS: Earnings attributable to each share.
  • ROE: Profitability relative to shareholder equity.
  • Debt/Equity: A measure of debt compared with equity.
TECHNICAL ANALYSIS

Understanding Price Data

Technical analysis studies price and volume patterns over time.

  • RSI: A momentum indicator.
  • Moving Average: Smooths price data over a period.
  • MACD: A trend and momentum indicator.
  • Volume: The number of shares traded.
ANALYSIS CHECKLIST

Questions to ask when studying a stock

  1. What products or services does the company provide?
  2. Is revenue growing consistently?
  3. How much debt does the company have?
  4. How does its valuation compare with similar companies?
  5. What risks could affect its business?
